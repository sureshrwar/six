

Ok, I just have had enough of x86. So far I have been porting
this stuff back and forth across Intel and x86 - but don't
have any more patience or energy. To make matters worse, the
only x86 machine that I have access to, it's damn slow, messy,
and has innumerable problems. Which now leads me to realize
that it will be a better use of time if I concentrate just on
SPARC. In short, I'm leaving the x86 trail at a point where
the whole thing compiles on x86, but with various runtime bugs,
yet capable of loading something from the disk and running it.
So I thought of putting in this bare shell, hardly anything
more than a while loop, so anyone adventurous can ad-venture
into trying out running simple stuff.

In short - no init, no getty, no login, no nothing - all you
have is an ultra-simple shell which sits in a while loop and
attempts to execve() whatever you type in. Till it reads in
an "exit". Then it dies, and you are left alone with our init
task and its idle loop. It can't be, it can't end like this,
but it just did.
